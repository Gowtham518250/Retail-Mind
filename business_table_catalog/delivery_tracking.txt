TABLE: delivery_tracking


PURPOSE: Timestamped delivery status updates and optional staff notes/location for a delivery.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Tracking event ID.
- delivery_id (integer, required): Parent deliveries.id; deleting parent cascades.
- status (enum, required): PENDING, OUT, DELIVERED, FAILED, RETURNED.
- status_timestamp (datetime): Time of status update.
- staff_name (string, nullable): Staff name recorded for the update.
- notes (text, nullable): Status notes; may contain personal details.
- location_lat (float, nullable): Latitude recorded for update.
- location_lng (float, nullable): Longitude recorded for update.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- delivery_id -> deliveries.id; apply the delivery's shop scope for tenant isolation.
- A delivery may have multiple status events. For current status, select the latest event by status_timestamp, not any matching historical status.
- Location coordinates and notes are sensitive; do not include in general summaries.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: delivery status, tracking, dispatched, out for delivery, delivered, failed delivery, returned, latest status update.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- Which deliveries are currently out for delivery?
- How many deliveries were completed today?
- What is the latest status of a delivery?
