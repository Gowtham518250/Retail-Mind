TABLE: deliveries


PURPOSE: Home delivery jobs linked to a shop, customer, and invoice.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Delivery ID.
- shop_id (integer, nullable): Reference to shop_profiles.id.
- customer_id (integer, nullable): Reference to customers.id.
- invoice_id (integer, nullable): Reference to invoices.id.
- delivery_address (text, required): Destination address; sensitive personal data.
- delivery_date (date, nullable): Scheduled delivery date.
- delivery_time (string, nullable): Scheduled time in HH:MM format.
- assigned_to (string, nullable): Delivery staff name.
- special_instructions (text, nullable): Customer/delivery instructions; may be sensitive.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- customer_id -> customers.id; invoice_id -> invoices.id; shop_id -> shop_profiles.id.
- This table has no status column. Use delivery_tracking for delivery status.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Use delivery_date for scheduled-delivery questions.
- Restrict by shop and do not expose address or instructions unless needed and authorized.
- Join delivery_tracking for the latest status; this table alone cannot answer pending/delivered status.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: home delivery, delivery schedule, delivery date, assigned delivery, delivery address, delivery invoice, delivery customer.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How many deliveries are scheduled today?
- Which invoices have a delivery scheduled this week?
- Who is assigned to a delivery?
