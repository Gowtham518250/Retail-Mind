TABLE: khata_history


PURPOSE: Individual credit-ledger events for khata balances, including invoice/credit and payment transaction types.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): History record ID.
- khata_id (integer, required): Parent khata_balances.id; deleting the parent cascades to history.
- transaction_type (enum, required): INVOICE, PAYMENT, ADJUSTMENT.
- amount (numeric 12,2, required): Transaction amount.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- khata_id -> khata_balances.id -> khata_balances.shop_id for shop scope.
- This model has no transaction timestamp, reference ID, or notes column; do not promise chronological history or invoice-level linkage from this table alone.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Group amount by transaction_type after applying shop scope through khata_balances.
- Do not assume whether PAYMENT amounts are stored as positive or negative; inspect the ledger service before calculating net balance from history.
- For the current balance, use khata_balances.khata_balance unless the application explicitly defines history as the source of truth.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: khata history, credit transaction, ledger entry, invoice on credit, khata payment, balance adjustment.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How much was recorded as khata payments?
- Break down khata transactions by type.
- Show the ledger entries associated with a customer's khata balance.
