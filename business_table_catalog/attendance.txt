TABLE: attendance


PURPOSE: Daily employee/worker attendance, check-in and check-out timestamps, attendance status, and calculated working hours.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Attendance record ID.
- employee_id (integer, required): Reference to user_details; use only as an internal staff reference and do not retrieve account/authentication details.
- worker_id (integer, nullable): Reference to workers.id when the record belongs to a managed worker.
- attendance_date (date, required): Attendance business date.
- check_in_time (datetime, nullable): Check-in timestamp.
- check_out_time (datetime, nullable): Check-out timestamp.
- status (enum, default ABSENT): PRESENT, ABSENT, LEAVE, HALF_DAY, LATE.
- working_hours (float, default 0): Recorded/calculated hours.
- notes (text, nullable): Attendance notes; may contain personal information.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- worker_id -> workers.id when provided.
- employee_id refers to an account record; avoid joining or returning user profile/authentication information.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Filter by attendance_date for day/week/month attendance questions.
- Count records grouped by status; calculate hours with SUM(working_hours).
- Do not assume one row per person per day across all worker identifiers; respect the table's uniqueness rule on employee_id, attendance_date, worker_id.
- Restrict access to authorized shop staff data; avoid exposing free-text notes.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: attendance, present, absent, late, half day, leave, check in, check out, hours worked, attendance date.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How many staff were present today?
- What are total working hours this week?
- Show attendance counts by status this month.
