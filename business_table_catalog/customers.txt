TABLE: customers


PURPOSE: Customer records used for customer counts, customer lookup, location-based summaries, credit terms, and invoice relationships. This is business customer data, not user-account/authentication data.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Unique customer ID.
- user_id (integer, required, indexed): Shop/owner scope. Restrict customer queries to the authenticated shop; do not join to user account details for ordinary business questions.
- customer_name (string, required): Customer name.
- email (string, nullable): Customer email address.
- phone (string, required, indexed): Customer phone number.
- whatsapp_number (string, nullable): WhatsApp contact number.
- address (text, nullable): Street/postal address.
- city (string, nullable): City.
- state (string, nullable): State.
- postal_code (string, nullable): Postal/ZIP code.
- credit_limit (numeric 10,2, default 0): Maximum allowed credit.
- payment_terms (string, nullable): Terms such as Net 30 or COD.
- contact_preference (enum, default EMAIL): EMAIL, WHATSAPP, CALL, or SMS.
- is_active (boolean, default true): False indicates an archived customer.
- created_at (datetime): Record creation timestamp.
- updated_at (datetime): Last update timestamp.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- customers.id -> invoices.customer_id (one customer may have many invoices).
- To calculate customer purchase totals, join invoices on customer_id and aggregate invoice totals; do not assume a sales.customer_id exists.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Customer count: COUNT(*) over customers, normally WHERE is_active = true and user_id = :shop_id.
- Customer purchase history/spend: join invoices by customer_id and aggregate invoice data, applying the shop scope.
- "Top customers" is ambiguous: clarify or infer by invoice revenue (SUM(invoices.total_amount)) versus invoice count; exclude cancelled invoices when appropriate.
- Filter location questions using city, state, and postal_code.

Privacy and access note: The catalog describes customer contact columns but contains no customer records. Only retrieve email, phone, WhatsApp number, or full address when the user's request and access allow it; avoid returning these fields in aggregate answers.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: customer, buyer, client, top customer, customer count, customer spend, city, state, address, credit limit, payment terms, contact preference.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How many active customers do I have?
- Who are my top customers by invoice value this month?
- How much does a specific customer owe?
- How many customers are in a particular city?
