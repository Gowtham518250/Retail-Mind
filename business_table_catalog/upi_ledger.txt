TABLE: upi_ledger


PURPOSE: UPI collection records used for payment collection and reconciliation.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Ledger record ID.
- shop_id (integer, nullable): Reference to shop_profiles.id.
- invoice_id (integer, nullable): Reference to invoices.id.
- upi_id (string, nullable): Merchant UPI identifier receiving funds; sensitive financial identifier.
- amount (numeric 10,2, required): UPI transaction amount.
- upi_reference (string, nullable, unique): UPI transaction reference; sensitive identifier.
- customer_upi (string, nullable): Payer UPI identifier; personal/financial data.
- status (enum, default PENDING): PENDING, CONFIRM, FAILED, REFUND.
- payment_date (datetime): Payment timestamp.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- shop_id -> shop_profiles.id; shop_profiles.shop_id identifies the owning shop.
- invoice_id -> invoices.id; use invoice ownership to validate scope when joining.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Sum confirmed amounts only when the business definition requires successful collections; status values are CONFIRM, not a generic PAID label.
- Use payment_date for date filters.
- Avoid returning upi_id, customer_upi, or upi_reference in ordinary answers.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: UPI, UPI collection, UPI payment, online collection, UPI reconciliation, payment status, UPI amount.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How much UPI payment was confirmed today?
- How many UPI transactions failed this week?
- What UPI collections are linked to a specific invoice?
