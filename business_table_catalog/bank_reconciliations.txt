TABLE: bank_reconciliations


PURPOSE: Daily comparison between expected UPI collections and the actual amount deposited in the bank.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- shop_id scopes the record to a shop account; it references user_details.id.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Reconciliation ID.
- shop_id (integer, required): Shop scope; filter by authenticated shop.
- recon_date (date, required): Reconciliation business date.
- expected_upi_amount (numeric 12,2, default 0): Expected UPI amount.
- actual_bank_deposit (numeric 12,2, default 0): Actual deposited amount.
- status (enum, default PENDING): MATCHED, DISCREPANCY, PENDING.
- notes (text, nullable): Reconciliation notes; may include sensitive details.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Difference = actual_bank_deposit - expected_upi_amount; label the sign clearly.
- Filter by recon_date and shop_id.
- Count or group by status to identify pending/discrepant reconciliations.
- Do not infer transaction-level details; this is a daily summary table.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: bank reconciliation, bank deposit, expected UPI, actual deposit, mismatch, discrepancy, matched, reconciliation date.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- Which bank reconciliations have discrepancies?
- What was the expected and actual UPI deposit yesterday?
- How many daily reconciliations are still pending?
