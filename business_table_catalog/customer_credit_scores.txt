TABLE: customer_credit_scores


PURPOSE: Customer credit-risk indicators and suggested limits used for credit decisions.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Score record ID.
- customer_id (integer, required): Reference to customers.id.
- credit_score (integer, default 50): Score on a 0-100 scale.
- score_badge (enum, default REGULAR): CAUTION, REGULAR, TRUSTED.
- suggested_credit_limit (numeric 10,2, default 0): Suggested limit.
- total_purchases (integer, default 0): Purchase count/measure recorded by the scoring service; verify its meaning before treating as currency.
- on_time_payments (integer, default 0): Recorded on-time payment factor.
- late_payments (integer, default 0): Recorded late-payment factor.
- days_since_last_purchase (integer, default 999): Recency factor.
- avg_days_to_pay (float, default 0): Average payment-delay factor.
- last_calculated (datetime, nullable): Last scoring calculation time.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- customer_id -> customers.id -> customers.user_id for shop scope.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Present these as application-generated indicators, not as a guarantee of repayment or formal credit advice.
- Do not infer metric units beyond the model/service definition.
- Avoid exposing customer contact details in score summaries.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: customer credit score, credit risk, trusted customer, caution badge, suggested credit limit, late payments, payment history.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How many customers are marked TRUSTED?
- Which customers have a low credit score?
- What suggested credit limits were calculated recently?
