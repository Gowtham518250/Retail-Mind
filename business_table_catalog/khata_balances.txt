TABLE: khata_balances


PURPOSE: Current customer credit/khata balance maintained by shop and customer phone.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Khata balance ID.
- shop_id (integer, required, indexed): Shop scope; restrict every query to the authenticated shop.
- customer_phone (string, required, indexed): Customer lookup key; treat as personal contact data.
- customer_name (string, nullable): Customer display name.
- khata_balance (numeric 12,2, default 0): Current outstanding khata amount.
- last_transaction (datetime): Timestamp of last transaction, defaults to current time.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- khata_history.khata_id -> khata_balances.id.
- This table identifies customers by phone and has no declared FK to customers.id.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Outstanding khata = SUM(khata_balance), scoped to shop_id.
- Use customer_name or authorized phone lookup to locate a customer's balance; avoid returning phone numbers in aggregate answers.
- Do not combine this balance with invoice outstanding totals without checking whether the same debt is represented in both systems.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: khata, customer credit, credit ledger, outstanding customer balance, amount owed, customer dues, last transaction.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- What is the total outstanding khata balance?
- How much does a customer owe in khata?
- Which customer balances are overdue or highest?
