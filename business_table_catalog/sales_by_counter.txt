TABLE: sales_by_counter


PURPOSE: Associates invoice sales with a shop counter and staff name for counter-level sales reporting.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- shop_id -> shop_profiles.id.
- counter_id -> billing_counters.id.
- invoice_id -> invoices.id.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Counter-sale record ID.
- shop_id (integer, nullable): Reference to shop_profiles.id.
- counter_id (integer, nullable): Reference to billing_counters.id.
- invoice_id (integer, nullable): Reference to invoices.id.
- staff_name (string, nullable): Staff name recorded for the sale.
- counter_number (integer, nullable): Counter number.
- sale_date (date, nullable): Sale business date.
- sale_amount (numeric 10,2, nullable): Amount attributed to this counter sale.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Filter by shop_id and sale_date.
- Use COUNT(*) for recorded counter-sale rows and SUM(sale_amount) for recorded counter revenue.
- This table may overlap invoice/sales reporting. Do not add its amounts to invoice or sales totals without verifying whether it duplicates them.
- Avoid using billing counter credentials; this file describes only the business reporting surface.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: counter sales, sales by staff, counter number, staff sales, sale amount by counter, cashier performance.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- Which counter recorded the highest sales today?
- What were sales by staff member this week?
- How many invoices were processed at each counter?
