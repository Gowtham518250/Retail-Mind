TABLE: inventory


There is no model table literally named "inventory". Inventory information is represented by these tables:
1. products: current product catalog and stock snapshot.
2. stock_movements: dated stock change history.
3. product_batches: batch quantities and manufacture/expiry dates.

TABLE: products
PURPOSE: Product master data and current stock.
COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Product ID.
- user_id (integer, required, indexed): Shop/owner scope; use as tenant filter without retrieving user profile data.
- product_name (string, required): Product name.
- sku (string, required, indexed): Stock keeping unit; unique per shop with user_id.
- description (text, nullable): Product description.
- current_stock (numeric 12,3, default 0): Current stock snapshot.
- min_stock (integer, default 10): Low-stock alert threshold.
- max_stock (integer, default 100): Target maximum stock.
- reorder_level (integer, default 20): Reorder trigger level.
- unit_price (numeric 10,2, required): Selling price per unit.
- purchase_price (numeric 10,2, default 0): Cost price per unit.
- category (string, nullable, indexed): Product category.
- is_active (boolean, default true, indexed): Product active/archive state.

TABLE: stock_movements
PURPOSE: History of stock changes.
COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Movement ID.
- product_id (integer, required): Related products.id.
- movement_type (enum, required): IN, OUT, ADJUSTMENT.
- quantity (numeric 12,3, required): Quantity recorded for the movement.
- reason (string, nullable): Such as Purchase, Sale, Damage, or Inventory Adjustment.
- reference_id (string, nullable): External reference, e.g. invoice or purchase order ID.
- created_at (datetime): Movement timestamp.

TABLE: product_batches
PURPOSE: Batch-level stock and expiry tracking.
COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Batch ID.
- product_id (integer, required): Related products.id.
- batch_number (string, nullable, unique): Batch identifier.
- manufacture_date (date, nullable): Manufacture date.
- expiry_date (date, nullable): Expiration date.
- quantity (numeric 12,3, nullable): Quantity in the batch.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Current stock / low stock: query products.current_stock, usually include only is_active = true and scope by user_id.
- Low-stock meaning: compare current_stock to the requested threshold; common alert rule is current_stock <= reorder_level, but confirm the application's business rule before treating min_stock and reorder_level as interchangeable.
- Stock history: query stock_movements by product_id and created_at; IN/OUT/ADJUSTMENT are movement types.
- Expiring stock: query product_batches.expiry_date and quantity, joined to products for product name and shop scope.
- Do not add stock_movements.quantity blindly to current_stock: current_stock is already a snapshot and movements are history. Movement sign conventions must be verified before calculating a balance from movements.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- stock_movements.product_id -> products.id.
- product_batches.product_id -> products.id.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: inventory, stock, current stock, low stock, reorder, reorder level, SKU, product quantity, stock in, stock out, adjustment, movement history, batch, expiry, expiring.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- Which active products are below their reorder level?
- What is the current stock for a SKU?
- What stock movements happened today?
- Which product batches expire next month?
