TABLE: gift_cards


PURPOSE: Digital gift cards issued by a shop, including original/current balances, expiry, and active status.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- shop_id scopes the gift card to a shop account; it references user_details.id.
- No customer foreign key is declared for issued_to.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Gift card record ID.
- shop_id (integer, required): Shop/owner reference; apply authenticated shop scope.
- card_code (string, required, unique): Gift card code. Treat as sensitive; do not reveal unless explicitly authorized and necessary.
- initial_balance (numeric 10,2, required): Value when issued.
- current_balance (numeric 10,2, required): Remaining balance.
- issued_to (string, nullable): Recipient name.
- issued_date (datetime): Issuance timestamp.
- expiry_date (date, nullable): Expiration date; null means no expiry date is recorded.
- is_active (boolean, default true): Active/inactive flag.
- status (enum, default ACTIVE): ACTIVE, REDEEMED, EXPIRED.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Remaining gift-card liability/balance: SUM(current_balance) across the requested active/status scope; clarify whether zero balances are included.
- Issued value: SUM(initial_balance), not current_balance.
- Gift-card count: COUNT(*) filtered by shop_id, status, and/or is_active.
- Expiring cards: filter expiry_date within a date range; exclude null expiry_date.
- Do not expose card_code in ordinary summary answers.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: gift card, voucher, gift certificate, card balance, remaining balance, issued value, expiry, expiring, active card, redeemed, expired.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How many active gift cards are there?
- What is the total remaining balance on active gift cards?
- How much gift-card value was issued this month?
- Which gift cards expire this month?
