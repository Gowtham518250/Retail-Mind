TABLE: workers


PURPOSE: Shop-managed staff records used for staff roster and attendance association.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Worker ID.
- shopkeeper_id (integer, required): Shop/owner scope; do not retrieve owner account data.
- name (string, required): Worker name.
- phone (string, nullable): Personal contact data.
- address (text, nullable): Personal address.
- salary (numeric 10,2, default 0): Compensation data; restricted.
- assigned_work (string, nullable): Work assignment.
- position (string, default Staff): Role/position label.
- join_date (date): Join date.
- status (string, default active): Examples active, inactive, suspended.
- pin (string, nullable): Attendance access PIN; secret credential.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- attendance.worker_id -> workers.id.
- Never embed or retrieve pin values. Restrict phone, address, and salary to authorized requests; omit these from general staff summaries.
- Scope by shopkeeper_id.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: workers, staff roster, employee count, position, assigned work, active staff, join date, attendance worker.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How many active workers are there?
- How many staff members are assigned to each position?
- Which workers joined this year?
