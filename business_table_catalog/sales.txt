TABLE: sales


PURPOSE: Records product sale lines for shop sales reporting, units sold, and revenue.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Unique sale-row ID.
- shopkeeper_id (integer, required, indexed): Shop/owner scope for the sale. Use it to restrict a query to the authenticated shop; do not expose owner account data.
- product_name (string, required): Name of the sold product as recorded on the sale.
- price (numeric 10,2, required): Per-unit sale price.
- quantity (integer, required): Units sold on this sale row.
- total (numeric 10,2, required): Total value of this sale row.
- sale_date (date, indexed): Business date of the sale.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- "How many sales?" means COUNT(*) sale rows unless the user explicitly asks for items/units.
- "How many items/units sold?" means SUM(quantity).
- "Revenue / sales amount" means SUM(total), not COUNT(*) and not SUM(price).
- "Average sale value" means AVG(total) over sale rows.
- "Sales today" filters sale_date to the shop's current local business date.
- "Best-selling product" groups sale rows by product_name and sums quantity; for highest revenue, sum total instead.

Useful query concepts (parameterize values and enforce shop scope):
- Today's row count: COUNT(*) WHERE sale_date = :business_date AND shopkeeper_id = :shop_id.
- Today's revenue: SUM(total) with the same filters.
- Today's units: SUM(quantity) with the same filters.
- Product ranking: GROUP BY product_name, ORDER BY SUM(quantity) DESC.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- This table does not have a product_id or invoice_id. Product matching is by product_name only.
- shopkeeper_id is a tenant-scope reference, not a request to retrieve user profile/authentication data.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: sale, sales, sold, transaction, revenue, turnover, amount, quantity, units, daily sales, today, sale date, best seller, top product.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How many sales did my shop make today?
- What was today's sales revenue?
- How many units were sold this week?
- Which product sold the most by quantity this month?
- Which product generated the most sales value?
