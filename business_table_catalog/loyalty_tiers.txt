TABLE: loyalty_tiers


PURPOSE: Shop-specific loyalty level definitions and their point/discount thresholds.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Tier ID.
- user_id (integer, required): Shop/owner scope; restrict queries to the authenticated shop.
- tier_name (string, required): Tier label, e.g. Bronze, Silver, Gold.
- tier_level (integer, required): Ordered tier level.
- min_points (integer, default 0): Minimum points needed for the tier.
- discount_percentage (float, default 0): Discount percentage for the tier.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- customer_loyalty.current_tier_id -> loyalty_tiers.id.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Use this table to explain tier rules and discount thresholds, not customer transaction totals.
- Compare points against min_points only according to the application's tier-upgrade logic.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: loyalty tier, tier level, bronze, silver, gold, points threshold, reward discount, loyalty program.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- What are the loyalty tiers and their discounts?
- How many points are needed for Gold tier?
- What discount does each tier receive?
