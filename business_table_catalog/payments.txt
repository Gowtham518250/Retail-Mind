TABLE: payments


PURPOSE: Individual payment transactions recorded against invoices. Use this table for collected-payment reporting, not invoice billed revenue.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Payment record ID.
- invoice_id (integer, required): Related invoices.id; invoice deletion cascades to payments.
- payment_method (enum, required): CASH, CARD, TRANSFER, CHEQUE, ONLINE.
- amount (numeric 10,2, required): Amount of this payment transaction.
- payment_date (datetime): Payment timestamp.
- reference_number (string, nullable): Bank/transaction/check reference.
- notes (text, nullable): Payment notes.
- idempotency_key (string, nullable, unique): Offline retry protection key; not a business metric.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- payments.invoice_id -> invoices.id.
- To enforce shop isolation, join invoices and filter invoices.user_id = :shop_id; payments has no direct shop/user column.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Amount collected: SUM(payments.amount), with the requested payment_date range and shop scope through invoices.
- Payment count: COUNT(*) payment records; this differs from invoice count.
- Payment methods: GROUP BY payment_method.
- Do not use invoices.total_amount as payments received; invoice value and collected payments are separate metrics.
- payment_date is a datetime; use the shop's local timezone boundaries for date-based requests.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: payment, payments, money received, collected, collection, cash, card, transfer, cheque, online payment, transaction, payment method, payment date.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How much payment did I receive today?
- How many payment transactions were recorded this week?
- What amount was collected by cash versus card?
- Show payments received for a particular invoice.
