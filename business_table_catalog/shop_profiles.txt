TABLE: shop_profiles


PURPOSE: Store identity, public-facing business details, location, and online-store settings.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Profile ID.
- shop_id (integer, required, unique): Owner/account reference; use only for shop scoping.
- shop_name (string, required): Business name.
- shop_tagline (string, nullable): Business tagline.
- shop_description (text, nullable): Business description.
- shop_type (string, default General): Store type.
- phone, email, website (nullable strings): Store contact channels.
- gst_number (string, nullable): Tax registration identifier; sensitive business identifier.
- logo_url, logo_file_path (nullable strings): Branding asset references.
- latitude, longitude (nullable floats): Store coordinates; location data.
- address, address_line1, address_line2, location, city, state, postal_code: Store location fields.
- upi_id (string, nullable), upi_ids (text, nullable): Merchant payment identifiers; sensitive.
- shop_categories (text, nullable): JSON-encoded categories.
- is_online_store_enabled (boolean, default false): Online store setting.
- is_active (boolean, default true): Profile activity state.
- pan_number, registration_number: Sensitive tax/registration identifiers.
- contact_person_name, contact_person_phone, contact_person_email: Contact-person details; personal data.
- color_primary, color_secondary, logo_version: Branding configuration.
- created_at, updated_at: Profile timestamps.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- shop_id references user_details.id, but this catalog is only for business profile lookup; do not retrieve account/authentication data.
- shop_settings.shop_id -> shop_profiles.id.
- Do not expose tax IDs, merchant UPI IDs, contact-person private details, or precise coordinates unless explicitly authorized and required.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: shop profile, business name, store type, shop address, store location, online store enabled, business contact, branding.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- What is my shop name and type?
- Is the online store enabled?
- What city is the shop located in?
