TABLE: customer_occasions


PURPOSE: Customer birthdays and other occasion dates used for optional occasion discounts.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Occasion record ID.
- customer_id (integer, required): Reference to customers.id.
- occasion_type (enum, required): BIRTHDAY, ANNIVERSARY, WEDDING, CUSTOM.
- occasion_date (date, required): Occasion date; model comment indicates annual MM-DD use.
- discount_percentage (float, default 10): Occasion discount percentage.
- last_notification_sent (datetime, nullable): Last notification timestamp.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- customer_id -> customers.id -> customers.user_id for shop scope.
- Occasion dates are personal data. Do not expose individual dates or customer identity unless specifically authorized; aggregate counts where possible.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Match upcoming occasions by month/day for recurring annual occasions; do not blindly compare full year if stored values use a placeholder year.
- Apply shop scope through customers.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: birthday, anniversary, customer occasion, upcoming occasion, occasion discount, wedding date, special offer.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How many customer occasions are coming up this month?
- What discount is configured for birthdays?
- Which occasion types are recorded?
