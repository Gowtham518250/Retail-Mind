TABLE: invoices


PURPOSE: Billing records, billed amounts, due dates, payment status, and customer invoice history.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- invoices.customer_id -> customers.id.
- invoice_line_items.invoice_id -> invoices.id.
- payments.invoice_id -> invoices.id.
- user_id scopes the invoice to a shop account; it references user_details.id.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Unique invoice ID.
- user_id (integer, required, indexed): Shop/owner scope. Always restrict invoice queries to the authenticated shop.
- customer_id (integer, nullable): Related customers.id.
- customer_name (string, nullable): Customer name snapshot on invoice.
- customer_phone (string, nullable): Customer phone snapshot on invoice.
- invoice_number (string, required, indexed): Display invoice number; unique per shop.
- offline_id (string, nullable, indexed): Offline-sync idempotency identifier; unique per shop when present.
- invoice_date (date, indexed): Invoice date.
- due_date (date, nullable): Payment due date.
- subtotal (numeric 10,2, default 0): Pre-tax subtotal.
- tax (numeric 10,2, default 0): Tax amount.
- total_amount (numeric 10,2, required): Final invoice amount.
- paid_amount (numeric 10,2, default 0): Amount recorded as paid.
- status (enum): DRAFT, SENT, PAID, OVERDUE, PARTIAL, CANCELLED.
- payment_status (enum, indexed): UNPAID, PARTIAL, PAID, OVERDUE.
- payment_method (string, nullable): Invoice payment method label.
- source (string, default MANUAL_ENTRY): Examples include OFFLINE_SYNC, ONLINE_ORDER, MANUAL_ENTRY.
- notes (text, nullable): Invoice notes.
- created_at (datetime): Creation timestamp.
- updated_at (datetime): Last update timestamp.

Related data:
- invoices.customer_id -> customers.id.
- invoice_line_items.invoice_id -> invoices.id, with line product, quantity, unit_price, and line_total.
- payments.invoice_id -> invoices.id, with individual payment amounts and dates.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Invoice count: COUNT(*) over invoices, scoped by user_id.
- Billed value: SUM(total_amount). This is not the same as cash received.
- Outstanding balance: Prefer SUM(total_amount - paid_amount) for eligible invoices; define whether cancelled/draft invoices are excluded. Use payment_status for status-based questions.
- "Unpaid invoices" usually filters payment_status = UNPAID; "overdue" filters payment_status = OVERDUE or due_date before the business date with an unpaid balance, depending on the reporting rule.
- Period filters should use invoice_date unless the user asks when an invoice was created (created_at).
- To answer item-level questions, join invoice_line_items; do not infer product quantities from invoice header totals.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: invoice, bill, billing, billed, total amount, subtotal, tax, due date, unpaid, partial, overdue, outstanding balance, invoice number, invoice date.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How many invoices are unpaid?
- What is the total outstanding invoice balance?
- How much did I invoice this month?
- Which invoices are overdue?
- Show the items on a specific invoice.
