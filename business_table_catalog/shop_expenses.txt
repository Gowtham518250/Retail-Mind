TABLE: shop_expenses


PURPOSE: Dated business expenses such as rent, salaries, utilities, and supplies. Use for expense totals and category breakdowns, not sales revenue.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- shop_id scopes the expense to a shop account; it references user_details.id.
- No category foreign key is declared; category is stored as text.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Expense ID.
- shop_id (integer, required): Shop/owner scope; apply authenticated shop filter.
- category (string, required): Expense category, e.g. rent, utilities, salary, supplies.
- amount (numeric 12,2, required): Expense amount.
- description (string, nullable): Expense details.
- expense_date (date, required): Business date of expense.
- payment_method (string, nullable): Payment method label.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Expense total = SUM(amount), filtered by shop_id and expense_date range.
- Group by category for category breakdowns.
- Do not treat this table as revenue or assume it includes all outflows unless the business flow confirms that.
- Profit requires a defined revenue source and expense policy; do not infer profit from expenses alone.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: expense, expenses, spending, operating cost, rent, utilities, salary expense, supplies, category, expense date.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How much did my shop spend today?
- What were expenses by category this month?
- How much was spent on utilities last month?
