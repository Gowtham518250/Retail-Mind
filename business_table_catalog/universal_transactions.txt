TABLE: universal_transactions


PURPOSE: Unified journal for shop money in and out, with a transaction type, category, amount, reference, and timestamp.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- shop_id scopes the transaction to a shop account; it references user_details.id.
- reference_id is a generic string and has no declared foreign key.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Transaction ID.
- shop_id (integer, required): Shop scope; restrict queries to the authenticated shop.
- tx_type (enum, required): INCOME or EXPENSE.
- category (string, nullable): Business category examples include SALE, KHATA_REPAY, EXPENSE, PO_PAYMENT, SALARY.
- amount (numeric 12,2, required): Transaction amount.
- reference_id (string, nullable): Related invoice/expense or other business record reference.
- description (string, nullable): Transaction description.
- tx_date (datetime): Transaction timestamp.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Filter by shop_id, tx_type, category, and tx_date as needed.
- Aggregate SUM(amount) by type/category for the values recorded in this journal.
- Do not assume this table is complete or deduplicated against sales, invoices, expenses, and purchase orders; verify write paths before combining totals across tables.
- Do not call INCOME "sales revenue" unless category/source confirms it.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: cash flow, money in, money out, income, expense, transaction journal, SALE, KHATA_REPAY, PO_PAYMENT, SALARY, tx date.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- What income and expenses were recorded this week?
- Show transaction totals by category this month.
- How much was recorded for purchase order payments?
