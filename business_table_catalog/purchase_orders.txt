TABLE: purchase_orders


PURPOSE: Supplier/wholesaler orders for inventory replenishment and procurement tracking.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- shop_id scopes the order to a shop account; it references user_details.id.
- No supplier or purchase-order line-item foreign keys are declared; supplier_name and items_json are stored on this row.

COLUMNS:
- id (integer, primary key): Purchase order ID.
- shop_id (integer, required): Shop/owner reference; apply authenticated shop scope.
- supplier_name (string, required): Supplier or wholesaler name.
- status (enum, default DRAFT): DRAFT, SENT, DELIVERED, CANCELLED.
- total_cost (numeric 12,2, default 0): Total purchase order cost.
- items_json (text, required): JSON-encoded items in the purchase order.
- expected_delivery (date, nullable): Expected delivery date.
- created_at (datetime): Purchase order creation timestamp.

QUERY GUIDANCE:
- Purchase order count: COUNT(*) filtered by shop and optionally status/date.
- Procurement cost: SUM(total_cost); this is purchase cost, not sales revenue.
- Expected deliveries: filter expected_delivery by date/range and status as appropriate.
- Parse items_json for product-level purchase details. No normalized purchase-order line-items table is specified in this model.

RETRIEVAL TERMS: purchase order, purchase, procurement, supplier, wholesaler, restocking, replenishment, total cost, expected delivery, delivered, draft, sent, cancelled.

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS:
- How many purchase orders are still drafts?
- What is the total procurement cost this month?
- Which purchase orders are expected to arrive this week?
- Which supplier has the most open purchase orders?
